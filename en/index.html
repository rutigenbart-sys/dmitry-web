Tip

How to choose a domain

A domain barely affects rankings directly, but it affects memorability and trust.

  • Pick a short memorable name in .ru — it is easier to type and opens more reliably in messengers.
  • A brand beats a long keyword: it is remembered, while a keyword-stuffed domain looks like spam.
  • At most one hyphen, no digits, no complex words.
  • Register it in your name and enable auto-renewal — it is your asset. About 500 ₽ per year.
